Comparing the two 1600×1000 renders, the compositions are close but several concrete offsets and finishes diverge. Fixes ranked by visual impact:

1. **AuthKit wordmark is undersized.** Reference cap-height of "A" is ≈125px (wordmark spans ~465px wide). Current render is ≈95–100px tall / ~440px wide — noticeably shorter. The CSS `font-size:clamp(88px, 9vw, 150px)` yields ~144px at 1600px; raise to `clamp(104px, 11.4vw, 190px)` so the effective size is ~180px. This is the single biggest delta.

2. **Wordmark gradient too pale/uniform.** Reference top is ice-white (#eaf3fe) and bottom sinks to a distinct soft blue (#8fb8ea), giving a lit-from-behind look. Current `linear-gradient(180deg,#e2eefc,#c3d8f5 48%,#98c0ef)` reads flatter and lighter overall. Push stops to `0% #eef6ff, 42% #cfe0f7, 100% #8ab4e6` and remove the ±1px vertical blur from the ::before halo (widen to ~140% × 200%, opacity .18).

3. **Card fan sits ~10px too low and content is over-stretched.** Reference center-card top edge ≈ y=525; current ≈ y=535. `margin:64px auto 0` → `54px`. Inside the center card, Email label / input / Continue in the reference sit tighter (label y≈650, CTA y≈730); current has them ~25–30px further down because `card-field{margin-top:22px}` + 44px top padding is too generous — reduce to `margin-top:16px` and card padding `40px 32px 22px`.

Drop to `filter:brightness(.92)` and raise their card title-`color` from `#eaf1fc` to `#eef4fd` so "Welcome to" / "Enter your code" carry the same small luminance as the reference.

Current `.hero-haze` is width 1120px but opacity .35 with a cool tint — raise opacity to `.5`, use `rgba(200,220,248,.28)→transparent`, and extend height to 720px.
