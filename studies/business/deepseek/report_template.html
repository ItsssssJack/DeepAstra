Definitions. Net revenue = gross − discounts − matched refunds. Contribution = net revenue − COGS − fulfilment. ROAS = net revenue ÷ ad spend; it is neither profit nor incremental marketing lift, and attribution is correlational. Ad spend covers every date in the period. Q3 = July–September, Q4 = October–December. No extrapolation to other periods.
Method. Duplicate order_id rows are identical and collapsed to one; only status = paid orders count. Refunds are matched to their original order and attributed to that order's date, market and channel. All arithmetic runs on integer cents.
Fictional data. Synthetic commerce dataset — not a real business. Figures reproduced from results.json by analysis.py.
