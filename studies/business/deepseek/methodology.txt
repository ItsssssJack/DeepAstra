METHODOLOGY
Synthetic commerce dataset, 2025-07-01 to 2025-12-31, USD. Fictional data, not a real business.

INPUTS
  orders.csv    raw order rows with status, gross_cents, discount_cents, cogs_cents, fulfilment_cents
  refunds.csv   refund_id, order_id, amount_cents
  ad_spend.csv  daily spend_cents per channel
All money is integer cents. No floating point is used for any stored value.

STEPS (analysis.py)
1. Deduplicate orders by order_id. Duplicate rows in this dataset are identical
   (verified: 250 duplicate rows, 0 conflicting groups), so one row per order_id is kept.
2. Keep only status == "paid". Cancelled and pending orders are excluded from every metric.
3. Match refunds to their order_id and attribute each refund to the ORIGINAL order's
   date, country and channel. Refunds on non-paid orders are excluded (0 in this dataset).
4. Net revenue    = gross_cents - discount_cents - matched refunds.
   Contribution   = net revenue - cogs_cents - fulfilment_cents.
   Contribution margin = contribution / net revenue.
5. ROAS = net revenue / ad spend, per channel. Ad spend is counted for every date in the
   period. Ad spend is never subtracted in the country contribution metric.
6. Quarters: Q3 = 2025-07-01..2025-09-30, Q4 = 2025-10-01..2025-12-31. No extrapolation.
7. Self-checks assert that net revenue reconciles across country, channel and quarter
   splits, that change_cents = q4 - q3, and that growth_pct is derived from the same values.

OUTPUTS
  results.json         rounded-free values in cents; growth_pct and margin_pct as percentages
  report_data.json     date x country x channel cube + date x channel spend, used by the report
  index.html           standalone report; data is inlined, no external requests

REPORTING NOTES
  * Revenue is not profit. Headline growth is net revenue; contribution is the profit-side
    measure and excludes ad spend by definition. Blended ROAS is shown separately and is
    never treated as profit.
  * Channel ROAS is correlational. Ad spend is not split by country, so applying a country
    filter lowers the ROAS numerator without lowering spend; the report states this.
  * The Q4 vs Q3 decline is explained by volume and refunds, not by order value:
    paid orders -309, refunds +$16,305, net AOV flat at $430 vs $429.
