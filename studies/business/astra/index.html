The rules behind the numbers.

Identical order IDs are deduplicated. Only paid orders are included. Every matched refund is allocated to the original order month.

Net revenue = gross − discounts − refunds. Contribution = net revenue − COGS − fulfilment. Country contribution never subtracts advertising costs. ROAS uses all recorded channel ad spend in July–December.

All source amounts are integer cents. Display rounding does not change calculations. This is a synthetic business, not a forecast or a claim of incremental marketing return.
