# DeepAstra presentation references

The approved reference is Jack’s September 10 presentation, “Build beautiful websites. With GPT-6 Astra.” Its source is retained in the September 10 workspace under outputs/astra-websites. This revision copies the exact local Inter and Newsreader font files, heading weights, letter spacing, centred heading and visual composition, dark background, subtle borders and quiet controls. It does not use the Claude Design presentation as the reference. Top navigation remains removed as requested.

The opening beam follows the visual reference https://huly.io/. Its GLSL comes from React Bits LaserFlow (https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/tree/main/src/content/Animations/LaserFlow), adapted to this presentation using native WebGL. Copyright and the MIT + Commons Clause license are retained in assets/react-bits-license.md. The shader runs a closed 24-second forward-and-return cycle, pauses offscreen and respects the motion control and reduced-motion preference. A static beam appears if WebGL is unavailable. The three choices describe allocation of work, not model blending or intelligence percentages.

DeepSeek always retains electric blue in the benchmark carousel. Other models are grey. The blue gradient moves within the bar; bar heights, labels, rankings and source values do not change with the animation. Motion pauses when offscreen or requested.

The retained blue glass film is used in the cost chart. It originated in the authorized Higgsfield generation. It uses an eased forward-and-return sequence with matching source frames at both endpoints. Compressed frame differences are in loop-validation.json. It is no longer layered behind the opening beam.

The three news icons come from Jack’s licensed Startups pack. Brand marks and the check animation come from his retained assets. These assets remain in the private review repository. The exact experiment outputs and numbers are unchanged.

The business assessment now has three mutually exclusive tabs: What passed, Judgment and Reports. Its palette and card treatment follow the Agentic OS Business screen. Original model reports remain untouched. Complete cost was not measured for that pair.
